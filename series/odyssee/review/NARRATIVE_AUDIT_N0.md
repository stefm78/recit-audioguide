## Sequence audit

### S01 — Une maison qui attend

**Function:** establish the damaged oikos, Penelope’s resistance, Telemachus’s unfinished adulthood and Athena’s push toward action.

**Strong ingredients**
- household economy is dramatized through wine, livestock, stores and occupation of space;
- Penelope’s weaving is both material action and strategy;
- mother/son relation is immediately specific;
- the suitors embody abusive consumption rather than merely being labelled villains.

**Weak / light ingredients**
- sensory palette is deliberately modest;
- service hierarchy and the legal/social structure of the household remain implicit.

**N0 finding:** strong. No demonstrated need for a society lecture.

### S02 — L’île où l’on pourrait rester

**Function:** make return a choice rather than merely a destination.

**Strong**
- bodily craft of building the raft;
- home is described through ordinary things rather than heroic abstraction;
- Calypso tests Ulysses’s desire, fear and changed identity.

**Light**
- the island remains archetypal rather than materially singular.

**N0 finding:** likely intentional. The dramatic contrast “perfect place / wrong home” matters more than ethnography.

### S03 — Nausicaa

**Function:** move Ulysses from survivor to social being and prepare Phaeacian hospitality.

**Strong**
- physical vulnerability;
- gender/reputation constraint is dramatized naturally through Nausicaa’s refusal to enter with him;
- Ulysses learns to use less rhetoric.

**Light**
- Scheria/Phæacian place identity is still generic;
- only a few material/civic markers distinguish this society.

**N0 finding:** possible high-value world enrichment, but not yet proven necessary.

### S04 — L’étranger qui pleure

**Function:** hospitality → song → tears → identity → ownership transfer.

**Strong**
- xenia/hospitality is already dramatized: food before biography, shelter and return passage;
- bardic culture is lived rather than explained;
- identity reveal is clean;
- transition to Ulysses storyteller is structurally excellent.

**Light**
- ritual/material details of hospitality are almost absent despite being available in the source tradition.

**N0 finding:** candidate for extremely small lived-detail enrichment only if it does not interfere with P2.

### S05 — Personne

**Function:** prove Ulysses’s metis, then reveal his destructive need for kleos/name.

**Strong**
- best-integrated world scene in the wanderings;
- cheese, milk, livestock, cave, stone, olive staff, wine, fire and rams all have dramatic jobs;
- hospitality law is tested through conflict, not lecture;
- sensory and spatial mechanics drive suspense;
- “Personne” / name motif pays forward into the whole work.

**N0 finding:** do not enrich. This is a reference scene for “world through action”.

### S06 — Ithaque en vue

**Function:** almost-return, trust failure, destruction of fleet identity, leadership guilt.

**Strong**
- sight of Ithaca is emotionally precise;
- Euryloque provides adversarial memory rather than obedient supporting dialogue;
- sail/wood/wind bodily cues work well;
- counting survivors makes leadership moral rather than abstract.

**Light**
- navigation and shipboard culture remain selective.

**N0 finding:** no defect proved. More seamanship detail could easily slow the scene.

### S07 — Circé

**Function:** transform “danger” from monster into comfort and delay.

**Strong**
- domestic comfort becomes the antagonist;
- companions recover bodies, sleep and food;
- Ulysses admits that he too learned to stay;
- “tomorrow” is used as a behavioral motif.

**Light**
- the household itself is generic;
- ritual/social texture is limited.

**N0 finding:** world enrichment is possible but low priority; the scene’s real subject is seduction by recovery.

### S08 — Ceux qui ne rentreront pas

**Function:** confrontation with limits, dead mother, knowledge that cannot repair loss.

**Strong**
- abstraction supports the artistic direction for the Underworld;
- Anticleia moves the scene from prophecy to personal loss;
- inability to touch is the material action.

**Apparently weak**
- world/civilization score is low.

**N0 finding:** **intentional low score, not a defect**. Adding topography or lore would likely damage the scene.

### S09 — Ce qu’Ulysse veut entendre

**Function:** connect Sirens directly to Ulysses’s need to be known.

**Strong**
- danger is epistemic/psychological, not “beautiful singing” cliché;
- rope and mast give the concept a body;
- Euryloque exposes privilege in Ulysses reserving knowledge for himself.

**Apparently weak**
- little cultural/material context.

**N0 finding:** intentional. Do not turn the Sirens into mythological exposition.

### S10 — Choisir ce qu’on perd

**Function:** leadership under impossible trade-off, mutiny of trust, hunger, final loss of crew.

**Strong**
- Scylla/Charybdis choice is ethically dramatized;
- hunger progressively changes the meaning of the prohibition;
- smell of slaughter before Ulysses sees the act is excellent sensory writing;
- the final disappearance of the crew is restrained.

**N0 finding:** no enrichment required. Protect pacing and moral ambiguity.

### S11 — Le récit est terminé

**Function:** return ownership to the external narrator and convert “adventure” back into “return”.

**Strong**
- decompression is deliberate;
- Alcinoos forces thematic compression;
- the simple ship home is an excellent anti-adventure;
- failure to recognise Ithaca pays off the gap between remembered and lived home.

**Light**
- Phæacian gift/ritual/social texture is mostly summarized.

**N0 finding:** optional world enrichment exists, but the simplicity of departure is itself an artistic asset.

### S12 — Personne, une seconde fois

**Function:** disguise, Eumaeus, xenia from below, father/son recognition, plan.

**Strong**
- excellent hospitality contrast with Cyclops;
- food, hearth, dogs, pig keeping and poverty create lived household texture;
- recognition with Telemachus rejects instant sentimental payoff;
- “Personne” is reused with a new purpose.

**Risk**
- longest sequence in the work at 1202 spoken words;
- the explicit line on hospitality plus narrator explanation comes close to stating the thesis twice.

**N0 finding:** pacing is a watch item; no cut authorised at N0.

### S13 — Ceux qui savent

**Function:** staged recognitions through Argos, Penelope’s interrogation and Eurycleia’s scar recognition.

**Strong**
- strongest blend of material culture and emotion before S15;
- dog, food, mantle, clasp, washing basin and scar all carry plot/identity;
- Penelope’s verification intelligence is dramatized;
- almost no world detail is decorative.

**N0 finding:** reference scene. Protect.

### S14 — L’arc

**Function:** objective competence test → identity reveal → action climax.

**Strong**
- the bow is a physical test rather than a speech;
- object tension is excellent;
- combat is intentionally compressed after the reveal;
- Telemachus’s “father” payoff lands after action.

**Weak**
- the contest’s objective material target is simplified to “tirer correctement”;
- this loses an iconic and highly economical source detail: the twelve axes.

**N0 finding:** strongest surgical enrichment candidate in the corpus.

### S15 — Le lit

**Function:** emotional climax; Penelope—not Ulysses—owns final verification.

**Strong**
- the bed/olive tree is simultaneously object, architecture, memory, marriage and identity proof;
- Ulysses nearly falls back into self-narration and stops;
- Penelope’s test cannot travel with the hero’s stories;
- mutual change over twenty years is acknowledged rather than erased.

**N0 finding:** **protect unchanged** unless a later human performance gate exposes a concrete oral problem.

## Cross-work findings

### 1. The work is not under-written dramatically

No structural rewrite is justified by N0.

The strongest global architecture remains:

absence → survival → self-narration → loss → return → verification.

### 2. “World lived” is the relative gap, but not everywhere

The corpus already excels when objects/culture are inseparable from action:

- loom;
- hospitality food;
- Cyclops’s cave and livestock;
- mast/rope;
- hunger/cattle;
- Eumaeus’s hearth;
- washing basin/scar;
- bow;
- bed/olive tree.

The weaker places are mainly transitions and some social settings, especially Scheria.

### 3. Religion is mostly plot-level, less lived-level

Gods are important characters/forces, but everyday ritual practice is lightly represented.

This is a real compositional observation, **not automatically a rewrite requirement**.

### 4. Social organization is selective

The script makes household authority, property consumption, kingship, servants and marriage stakes understandable enough for the plot.

It does not attempt a full representation of servitude/slavery, elite reciprocity or household labor.

Treating these as mandatory “coverage” would be documentary thinking, not dramatic thinking.

### 5. Exposition is generally controlled

Most cultural information is dramatized.

Rare near-thesis lines exist, especially in S12 around hospitality, but they are embedded in character voice and currently remain admissible.

### 6. The first-person middle does not become a monologue

Euryloque and other remembered voices repeatedly challenge Ulysses’s retrospective ownership.

This is one of the strongest protections against long-form monotony.

### 7. Main risk is not missing information; it is adding too much

The audit finds more opportunities than defects.

Therefore N1 must be aggressive in deleting candidate “improvements”.

## N0 verdict

**PASS_TO_N1 — NO_TEXT_CHANGE_AUTHORIZED_YET**

No non-compensable blocker found.

Primary headroom:
1. surgical world/civilization detail;
2. selective sensory specificity;
3. one iconic material restoration in S14.

Primary protection:
- do not enrich S05, S08, S09, S10, S13 or S15 merely because a category score is not maximal.
