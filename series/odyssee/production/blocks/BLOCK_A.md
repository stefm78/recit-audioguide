## Block dramatic job

Move from “Ulysse is an absence owned by other people” to “Ulysse says his name and takes ownership of the story”.

The block ends on:
- name revealed;
- narrative ownership transferred;
- Ulysse beginning “Nous avions quitté Troie…”.

No outro music.

## S01 — Une maison qui attend

Roles:
Narratrice, Antinoos, Télémaque, Eurymaque, Pénélope, Athéna.

Performance:
- Narratrice: observes pressure; no epic prologue.
- Pénélope: authority without volume; wit remains dry.
- Télémaque: young adult anger that is not yet command.
- Athéna: precise, human, close; never mystical.
- Pretenders: occupy space casually; avoid pantomime villainy.

Sound:
- primary: voices;
- supportive: domestic-banquet-light, small wood/ceramic activity, very light textile/loom when it serves Pénélope;
- acoustic space: dry or bounded interior only;
- forbidden: ominous score, divine reverb, constant crowd wall.

QA:
- listener understands that the house is being consumed, not merely visited;
- Pénélope and Télémaque are immediately distinct;
- Athéna remains distinct from Narratrice despite shared Denise provider;
- no pretender performance overwhelms the family conflict.

Transition S01→S02:
Subtract the crowded house. A short clean gap, then open shore. Do not crossfade banquet into generic ocean for several seconds.

## S02 — L’île où l’on pourrait rester

Roles:
Narratrice, Calypso, Ulysse.

Performance:
- Ulysse: tired, economical, not heroic;
- Calypso: warm and serious; staying must sound genuinely plausible;
- humour is conversational, never romantic-comedy scoring.

Sound:
- restrained near-shore presence only when useful;
- construction wood / rope punctual;
- several stretches may be voice + near-silence;
- storm is an event at the end, not a permanent bed;
- forbidden: lush paradise music, permanent sea loop.

QA:
- listener understands why Ulysse still chooses mortal Ithaque;
- Calypso is not reduced to seduction or imprisonment;
- storm escalation does not erase speech.

Transition S02→S03:
After breakage/storm, let violent detail fall away. Land is discovered by absence of being dragged back into the sea, not by a triumphant cue.

## S03 — Nausicaa

Roles:
Narratrice, Nausicaa, Jeune femme, Ulysse.

Performance:
- Nausicaa: young adult, cautious then decisive;
- Ulysse deliberately does less; social intelligence replaces command;
- companion is a brief cautioning presence, not comic fear.

Sound:
- beach/shore sparse;
- human distance matters more than ambience;
- no romantic music;
- no “magical rescue” sound.

QA:
- Nausicaa never sounds childlike;
- no romance is implied;
- Ulysse remains dangerous enough to justify caution but controlled enough to earn help.

Transition S03→S04:
Move from open shore to ordered human interior with one perceptible spatial change. No travel montage.
