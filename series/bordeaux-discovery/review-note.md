# Bordeaux — HUMAN_ROUTE_GATE

Open the generic review surface after a static build:

`reviews/route.html?slug=bordeaux-discovery`

The review shows the map, ordered stops, visit budget, indicative walking distance/time, per-leg estimates, editorial reasons and the ACCEPT / ADJUST / REJECT decision.

Current walking metrics are deliberately `INDICATIVE_NOT_ROUTED`. They support route selection but are not turn-by-turn routing evidence. Scripts, audio and final site publication remain blocked until the route is accepted.
