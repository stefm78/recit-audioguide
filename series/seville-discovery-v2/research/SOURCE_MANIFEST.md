## Source policy

- Prefer institutional / municipal / operator-primary sources for historical facts and current access information.
- Separate historical evidence from freshness-sensitive operating facts.
- When pages conflict about current access, prefer the most specific current page for the facility concerned and record the contradiction.
- A source may support research without implying that its venue should be added to the route.
- Revalidate dynamic hours, closures, event occupation and field visibility before materially later production if a scene depends on them.
- Clearly label legend, literary tradition, symbolic interpretation and promotional metaphor rather than promoting them to historical fact.
