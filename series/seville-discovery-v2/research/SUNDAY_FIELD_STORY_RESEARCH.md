### J1-SUN-001 — The irregular arena is not just “old”: its centre moved

- **Route section / location:** inside Maestranza, when the ruedo is visible.
- **What the visitor can observe:** the non-perfect geometry of the ring and the way seating/enclosure follow it.
- **Narrative hook:** the V1 already notices the irregularity; the stronger reveal is that eighteenth-century builders actually shifted the geometric centre and reduced the radius during later phases.
- **Concise factual basis:** Real Maestranza’s construction history explains that the 1761–1766 phase followed a more regular thirty-ochava circular concept, while the 1781–1785 phase displaced the centre and reduced the radius to fit the available plot and adapt dimensions as bullfighting on foot became dominant. The total stone-building process stretched to 1881.
- **Legend / tradition status:** `NONE`.
- **Sources:** `S39`.
- **Robustness:** `HIGH`; the shape is permanent. Do not ask the visitor to identify a mathematical centre by eye.
- **Suggested duration:** `45-90s`.

### J1-SUN-011 — Las Cigarreras: the city looks back at its own workers

- **Route section / location:** later museum rooms, if `Las Cigarreras` is on display.
- **What the visitor can observe:** Gonzalo Bilbao’s large 1915 factory scene, including workers and the grand tobacco-factory interior.
- **Narrative hook:** after three days of palaces, institutions and elite commissions, the museum can end with paid labour and women at work — though the painting itself also idealises its subjects.
- **Concise factual basis:** the museum describes `Las Cigarreras` (1915) as Bilbao’s homage to Seville’s cigar workers, showing everyday work in the Tobacco Factory; it notes that the figure of the cigarrera had been idealised in literature and opera.
